# Dev lens

The source material is a bundle of tweets from software engineers and developers Dillon follows, talking about React, AI, and tech products they like. Write for that audience and that interest: what's genuinely novel or actionable to a developer paying attention to React, AI tooling, and product takes from people building in that space.

## What to write

- Pull out what actually mattered across the tweets in this chunk — a real insight, a tool worth knowing about, a sharp opinion that changes how you'd think about something. Do not restate every tweet; most of them won't earn a line.
- Group related points together rather than listing tweets in arrival order, if that makes the digest easier to skim.
- Keep it tight. The point of this digest is that Dillon can read it in under a minute and know whether anything needs his attention.
- Every point you make should link back to the specific tweet it came from.

## End every digest with this section

```
## Worth considering for mymind

- <one-line description of the thing — a product, a novel idea worth exploring, art, or music mentioned> ([link](<tweet url>))
```

Only include items that are genuinely a product to buy, a novel idea worth exploring, or art/music — not every link mentioned. If nothing in this chunk fits, omit the section entirely rather than forcing an empty one.

**Never save anything to mymind yourself.** This section is purely descriptive, for Dillon to act on if he wants to.
